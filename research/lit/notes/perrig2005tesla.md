# Timed Efficient Stream Loss-Tolerant Authentication (TESLA): Multicast Source Authentication Transform Introduction (RFC 4082 2005)
url: https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc4082
read: abstract-only (RFC text fetched; only front matter and abstract inspected, section headings skimmed)
problem: Authenticate the source and integrity of each packet in multicast or broadcast streams with low per-packet cost.
method: TESLA, based on loose time synchronisation between receivers and source and delayed key disclosure (a key disclosure delay d, Section 3.6). Needs no trust between receivers, tolerates any level of loss without retransmission, no per-receiver state at the sender. Informational RFC, June 2005.
numbers: (none read)
limits: Per the abstract, no non-repudiation of the source to third parties; receivers must be loosely time-synchronised with the source; informational, not a standard.

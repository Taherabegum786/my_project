# SoK: Systematizing Hybrid Strategies for the Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography (IACR ePrint 2025/2052, 2025)
url: https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/2052
read: abstract-only
problem: Quantum computing threatens RSA and ECDH, and harvest-now-decrypt-later makes migration urgent. Hybrid classical/PQC (and QKD) techniques are a pragmatic way through the transition.
method: Review of hybrid cryptographic approaches and their integration into protocols such as TLS 1.3 and QUIC. Covers deployment challenges (performance trade-offs, security guarantees, compatibility with existing infrastructure), standardization-body and industry initiatives, and lessons from early adopters.

## Checked and found sound

- **Chain keys** come from `os.urandom(32)` per build (`modes.py:525`). The legacy public-seed derivation is reachable only through `legacy_public_seed=True` in negative test R8/C8 (`negative_tests.py:193`). No harness passes `chain_secret`. The round-3 Major 4 forgery is fixed.
- **Anchor coverage:** the anchor signs alg, epoch (32-bit), commitment, epoch start ts and the schedule (T, E, d, tag/key lengths, continuity) (`:100-105`, `:537`). The receiver takes the schedule only from a verified anchor and checks it against local policy (`:866-869`). Early and stale anchors are handled (`:870-874`).
- **Freshness:** frag/hyb sign (seq, ts, app); Merkle signs (batch, ts, root) with a per-leaf freshness check after the root is verified (`:758`). The chain checks staleness after the MAC (`:927`). There is a replay window of 64 messages.
- **Time-safety inequality:** with d = 2 and δ = 0.5 slot, honest frames pass for offsets ≤ +0.5 slot. `lossless_set` returns nothing when d < 1+δ (`:355`), consistent with the receiver.
- **Frame accounting:** header and per-symbol sizes match `mand` and `ov` for all three kinds. `put()` asserts the cap. The ≤255-symbols-per-slot limit holds, because u[t] ≤ 255 and the extras are bounded by u[t] - load[t] (`:244`, `:335`). The 255-symbol starvation fix looks correct.
- **Lossless invariant:** `lossless_invariant.py --quick` on the current tree (written to the scratchpad, not to `results/`) gives 2394 cells replayed, 1026 run with the real sender and receiver (873 feasible), 0 mismatches. Re-run it after the cv2x placement change lands.

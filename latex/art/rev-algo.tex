\begin{factsheet}
Recurrences & Master theorem: compare $f(n)$ with $n^{\log_b a}$ \\
Trees & $n_0 = n_2 + 1$; max nodes at height $h$: $2^{h+1} - 1$; null links $n + 1$ \\
Balanced trees & AVL minimum nodes 1, 2, 4, 7, 12, 20, 33; red--black height $\le 2\log(n+1)$ \\
Sorting & build-heap $O(n)$; comparison sort $\Omega(n\log n)$; min and max together $3n/2 - 2$ \\
Graphs & Dijkstra $O((V+E)\log V)$, no negative edges; Bellman--Ford $O(VE)$; Floyd $O(V^3)$; Kruskal $O(E\log E)$ \\
DP and strings & matrix chain $O(n^3)$; LCS $O(mn)$; 0/1 knapsack $O(nW)$; KMP $O(n+m)$ \\
Complexity & SAT was the first NP-complete problem (Cook); 2-SAT $\in$ P; halting is NP-hard but not in NP \\
\end{factsheet}

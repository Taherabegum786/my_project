\begin{formulas}
\begin{align*}
\text{EAT} &= (1-p)\times ma + p \times \text{page-fault service time}\\
\text{e.g. } ma = 200\text{ ns},\ \text{service} = 8\text{ ms},\ p = 0.001:\quad
\text{EAT} &\approx 200 + 0.001\times 8{,}000{,}000\text{ ns} \approx 8.2\ \mu\text{s}
\end{align*}
\end{formulas}
\noindent A fault rate of only one in a thousand slows memory down forty-fold; this is why page-fault rates
must be kept tiny.

\begin{formulas}
\begin{align*}
A^{+} &= \{A, B, C\}\\
(AD)^{+} &= \{A, D, B, C, E\} = \text{all attributes} \;\Rightarrow\; AD \text{ is a key}\\
\end{align*}
\end{formulas}
\noindent Since $A$ and $D$ never appear on a right-hand side, every key must contain both; $AD$ is therefore the only candidate key.

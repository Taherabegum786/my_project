\begin{formulatable}
Initial decision parameter & p_0 = 1 - r\quad\bigl(\text{or } \tfrac54 - r\bigr) \\
If $p_k < 0$: next pixel $(x_k+1,\ y_k)$ & p_{k+1} = p_k + 2x_{k+1} + 1 \\
Otherwise: next pixel $(x_k+1,\ y_k-1)$ & p_{k+1} = p_k + 2x_{k+1} + 1 - 2y_{k+1} \\
\end{formulatable}

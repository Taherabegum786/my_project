\textbf{Declaration} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\texttt{int *p;} & pointer to \texttt{int} \\
\texttt{int **pp;} & pointer to a pointer to \texttt{int} \\
\texttt{int *a[10];} & array of 10 pointers to \texttt{int} \\
\texttt{int (*a)[10];} & pointer to an array of 10 \texttt{int}s \\
\texttt{int *f();} & function returning a pointer to \texttt{int} \\
\texttt{int (*f)();} & pointer to a function returning \texttt{int} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\smallskip
{\small Read declarations from the name outwards: \texttt{()} and \texttt{[]} bind more tightly than \texttt{*}.}

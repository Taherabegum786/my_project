\begin{formulatable}
From the flow graph ($E$ edges, $N$ nodes, $P$ connected components, usually 1) & V(G) = E - N + 2P \\
From decisions & V(G) = \text{predicate nodes} + 1 \\
From the drawing (regions of the planar graph, outer region included) & V(G) = \text{number of regions} \\
Meaning & \text{independent paths} = \text{minimum basis-path test cases} \\
\end{formulatable}

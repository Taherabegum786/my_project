\noindent Initial values: \texttt{mutex = 1}, \texttt{empty = n}, \texttt{full = 0}. Swapping the two
\texttt{wait} calls in the producer can cause \textbf{deadlock}: it may hold \texttt{mutex} while waiting
on a full buffer, so the consumer can never enter to empty it.
